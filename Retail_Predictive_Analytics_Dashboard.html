Formulas used

Variance (sample) = Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / (n−1). Std. deviation = √variance. Coefficient of variation = std / |mean| × 100 — lets you compare volatility across metrics on different scales. % change compares the first vs. last week in the selected range. Rolling average uses a trailing 4-week window.

4 · Segmentation

Diagnostic · Where performance differs

Every split below is a transparent rule applied to the filtered data — the rule is stated above each table so the classification can be audited.

Store performance ?Stores are ranked by Sales Variance% (actual vs. budget) and split at the median: at/above median = High-performing, below = Low-performing.

Category growth ?Growth = (avg weekly sales in 2025 − avg weekly sales in 2024) / avg weekly sales in 2024 × 100. Positive = High-growth, negative = Declining.

Cost segments (by store) ?Expense ratio = Total Expense / Sales Revenue × 100. Above median = High-cost, at/below = Low-cost.

Promotion sensitivity (by category) ?Pearson correlation between weekly Discount Rate and weekly Transactions per category. r ≥ 0.30 = Promotion-sensitive.

    9 · Model Validation

    Uncertainty · How much to trust it

    Time-based train/test split: the model is fit only on the earliest 80% of weeks and scored on the most recent 20% it never saw — the honest way to validate a time-ordered business dataset (random shuffling would leak future information into training).
